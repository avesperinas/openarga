# Code of conduct

OpenArga adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be kind and assume good faith; welcome people of every background and level of
experience; criticise ideas and code, never people; no harassment, insults, sexualised language or
publishing anyone's private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and every
other space of the project, and to people and agents acting for someone alike.

If something happens that should not, report it privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** reaches them privately), or by any
private channel you have with them. Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may warn,
remove contributions or ban anyone who does not follow this code.
