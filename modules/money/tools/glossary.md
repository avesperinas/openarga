# Money glossary

- **Movement**: what a bank says about an entry, as it is (date, signed amount, text,
  counterparty). It is never touched; what it is gets decided apart and can be redone.
- **Transfer**: money between your accounts. Neither expense nor income. It is recognised by the
  counterparty's IBAN, by the bank's name in the text or by an opposite amount on a nearby date.
- **Nature**: expense or income. **Ownership**: individual (yours) or shared (both of you).
  **Category**: depends on both (there is an individual Travel and a shared one).
- **Refund**: an income that subtracts from the spending of its category (the transfer for a
  dinner subtracts from Leisure). It is not income.
- **Default**: the category the account sets when no rule decides; it stays marked for review.
- **To label**: cash; it does not count as spending until it is said what it went on.
- **Settlement**: a payment between you two that settles a part; neither expense nor income.
- **Return**: interest or yield of an account; neither expense nor income.
- **Shared**: the shared expenses. What is split in half counts half for each; the rest follows the
  declared incomes of the year (the ratio). The balance says who has put in more and is settled per
  calendar year.
- **Net worth**: what everything is worth except real estate, each account by your share.
  **Liquid**: net worth without what cannot be taken out at will (pension plans).
  **Cash**: current and savings accounts without the emergency fund, shared ones whole.
- **Emergency fund**: the account with that role; its target is the basic spending of *n* months.
- **Monthly spending**: average of up to 12 months of what is yours plus your part of the shared,
  without what is not living expense (Work). **Basic spending**: the same without what is not basic
  (Travel, Leisure).
- **Carried**: an account's value that comes from its last valuation, not from today.
- **Product**: any account of yours (current, savings, fund, pension plan, crypto, property,
  deposit). **Snapshot**: which funds a product held on a day; between snapshots each fund moves
  with its price. **Composition**: the portfolio split by asset class, region, fund or product;
  what cannot be split is "no breakdown".
- **Deposit**: your money held elsewhere that comes back (a rent deposit); neither savings nor
  spending.
