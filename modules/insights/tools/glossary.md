# Insights glossary

- **Advice** (an insight): something Insights tells you because it sees it in your data. It always
  carries one of your figures with its baseline, what it affects and an action. Without the three
  it is not advice, it is an opinion.
- **Baseline**: what is normal for you, not for people in general: your 90-day average, your usual
  band, your pace. "You sleep little" has no baseline; "6.1 h on average against your 7.2 h" does.
- **Alert** (`alert`): a single piece of advice, when something passes the bar. Most days there is
  none.
- **Summary** (`weekly`): Sunday's, about the whole week. It always comes out.
- **Topic** (`topic`): what advice is about, to avoid repeating it. It starts with the app the main
  figure comes from: `money.spend:restaurants`, `vitals.weight`, `cross.steps-weight`.
- **Rating**: `useful` it helped; `known` they knew it (raise the bar on that topic);
  `not_applicable` it does not apply (do not insist).
- **Trait**: something concrete about your tastes, with its stance (−2 to +2), where it comes from
  and its evidence. The AI proposes it (`propose_trait`); the person confirms it. What they say
  themselves is noted as confirmed (`note_taste`).
- **Pill**: a few minutes of reading on one of their topics, with sources really read, "to tell",
  "to think" and three branches. It talks about the world, never about their figures.
- **Fact of the day**: something true and surprising from their topics, with its source. Five a
  day.
- **Recommendation** (`pick`): something to read, watch or listen to, checked in a catalogue, with
  why for them. What does not appear in the catalogue is not published.
